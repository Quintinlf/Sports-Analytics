1 · Seven shuffles

Distance from a perfectly shuffled deck after m riffles

Exact total variation distance (Bayer & Diaconis, 1992): 0 means indistinguishable from random, 1 means an observer can tell for certain. It is also the biggest edge any betting strategy could squeeze out of the leftover order. Computed in exact integer arithmetic, not simulated.

2 · Watch a shuffle

Colour each card by where it started

A new deck is a smooth rainbow. A riffle interleaves two packets, so the rainbow survives as two interleaved runs. Count the rising sequences: a new deck has 1, a well-shuffled deck averages 26.5.

3 · A cut is a rotation

Cutting as a walk around the 52nd roots of unity

A cut moves the top j cards to the bottom: a rotation by j. Rotations add up mod 52, so repeated cutting is a random walk on ℤ₅₂. Its characters are the roots of unity ωjm, with ω = e2πi/52 (De Moivre).
